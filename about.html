A little about me

Curiosity has taken me in a few different directions.

I’m a PhD candidate in Transport and Planning at TU Delft, where I study crowd forecasting and risk prediction. I also have a background in computer vision, applied AI and biodiversity research.

Before moving to Delft, I worked as a senior computer vision engineer and AI consultant on document understanding, visual recognition and other applied systems. Outside work, I enjoy photography, astronomy, birdwatching and butterfly watching.

I co-founded The Nature and Butterfly Society in Tamil Nadu and have taken part in surveys, outreach, publications and the Pattampoochi butterfly magazine.
